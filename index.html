We exist to protect the voiceless, hold power accountable, and turn rights from paper promises into lived realities, for humans and animals alike.

One council, three divisions

Rights Are Not Separate Systems

To defend rights, prevent injustice, and drive systemic change through law, awareness, and global cooperation. The same failures harm people, animals and the land, so we keep all three under one legal framework and a case never falls between departments.

We are a rights institution, not a protest collective, a religious body, or a single-issue NGO.

Human Rights

01

We document and legally challenge violations against women and children under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, so that dignity and safety are enforced as rights rather than requested as favours.

Animal Rights

What We Act On

01 / 03
Division 01 · Women & Child Rights Human
Rights
Sexual violence and bodily integrity
Domestic and intimate-partner violence
Trafficking, forced labour and forced practices
Child abuse, child marriage and institutional neglect

Filed under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, alongside POCSO, the JJ Act and the PWDVA, and Articles 14, 15(3), 21 and 39(f) of the Constitution.

Division 02 · Cruelty & Wildlife Animal
Rights
Beating, poisoning and cruel killing
Unlawful culling and eviction of community animals
Illegal trade, transport and slaughter
Poaching, captivity and habitat destruction

Filed under the PCA Act 1960, the Wildlife Protection Act 1972 and BNS §325, and the constitutional duty of compassion, Article 51A(g).

Open the division ↗
Division 03 · Pollution & Land Environmental
Rights
Air, water and groundwater contamination
Illegal mining, felling and waste dumping
Coastal, wetland and mangrove destruction
Displacement without rehabilitation

Filed under the EPA 1986, the Air and Water Acts and before the National Green Tribunal, on the principle that the polluter pays.
