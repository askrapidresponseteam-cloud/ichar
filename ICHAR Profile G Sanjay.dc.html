Adv. G. Sanjay manages an independent practice in the name and style of Jay & Law Company in Coimbatore, handling diverse legal portfolios and delivering strategic litigation for clients in the Supreme Court, various High Courts and District Courts.

Early in the legal career came the privilege of working as a junior advocate under Retd. Justice Senior Advocate Hon'ble Mr. Justice R. Balasubramanian, until 2020.

Originally from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, Adv. Sanjay holds a B.E. in Electronics & Communication Engineering and an LL.B. from Lloyd Law College.
