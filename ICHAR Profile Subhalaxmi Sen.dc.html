Advocate Subhalaxmi Sen is a practising advocate before the Calcutta High Court and an animal-rights advocate committed to protecting animals from cruelty, exploitation, neglect and unlawful treatment. Her work brings together legal advocacy, animal rescue, rehabilitation and grassroots welfare, with a focus on securing protection, dignity and justice for voiceless beings.

Her legal advocacy includes matters arising under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960, the PCA (Care and Maintenance of Case Property Animals) Rules, 2017, and other applicable laws and regulatory frameworks concerning animal welfare, protection and transportation.

She has been involved in supporting animal-welfare causes before Courts, Police Authorities, Veterinary Authorities and Government and Administrative Bodies.

Beyond the courtroom

Compassion Into Action

Beyond the courtroom, she remains actively engaged in the care and protection of stray, abandoned and vulnerable animals.

Her approach is founded on the principle that compassion must translate into action, and that the rule of law should extend protection to every living being, particularly those incapable of speaking for themselves.

Protecting the voiceless. Defending the vulnerable. Respecting every living being.
