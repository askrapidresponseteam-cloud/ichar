Adv. Jothi Sathyap Priya V. practises before the Madras High Court, Chennai, holding a Bachelor of Business Management and a Bachelor of Laws. Her practice covers environmental law, constitutional and administrative law, civil litigation, public interest litigation and writ proceedings, alongside regulatory and statutory matters, legal research and drafting.

She has a particular interest in matters concerning environmental protection, pollution, natural resource conservation, regulatory compliance and the responsibilities of public authorities.

Her involvement in environmental matters has strengthened her understanding of the practical challenges faced by individuals, communities and institutions in seeking effective legal remedies.

Sound, and Fit for the Case

In her practice she strives to approach every matter with professionalism, integrity and perseverance, while seeking remedies that are legally sound and appropriate to the circumstances of each case.

Environmental matters rarely stop at one authority; the work is in tracing who owed the duty, and to whom.
