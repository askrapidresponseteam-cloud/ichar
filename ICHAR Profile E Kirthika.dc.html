Adv. E. Kirthika is a practising Advocate before the Madras High Court, with over three years of professional experience in legal practice. Her primary area of practice is Writ Petitions, with experience in civil, criminal and Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT) matters.

A proud Kendriya Vidyalaya alumna, she approaches her profession with integrity, diligence and respect for every individual. Her practice is guided by a commitment to understanding each matter carefully and upholding the principles of justice and fairness.

She is committed to contributing to society through her legal profession, public service and care for those in need.
